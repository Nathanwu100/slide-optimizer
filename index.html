Simplify your slides safely.

Your PowerPoint file is read locally for analysis. Review each AI suggestion, approve only the changes you want, and download a new copy.

No automatic edits SimplifyYourSlides never changes your original. It only creates a new copy after you explicitly approve formatting-safe suggestions. Mixed formatting and hyperlinks are left for manual review.
How to download your google slides presentation:
  1. Open the presentation in Google Slides.
  2. Click File.
  3. Select Download.
  4. Choose Microsoft PowerPoint (.pptx).
  5. Upload the downloaded .pptx file for read-only analysis.

    Our Story

    Founded in 2025, the Human Cognition and AI Club began with a simple idea: to create a space where people who are interested in learning and understanding the difference between AI and humans. What started as a small group of 13 enthusiasts meeting at our school has grown into a vibrant community of over 40 members.

    Our Mission

    We are here to empower people who are easily distracted or people with ADHD who struggle with focusing by providing SimplifyYourSlides to quickly change slides to more so fit our target people.
